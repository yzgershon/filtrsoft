Privacy at Filtrsoft

Updated September 13, 2026

Filtrsoft helps you research used technology. This page explains the data used by the current app. Contact yzgershon@gmail.com about your data or account.

Your account

Email signup stores your email, optional name and a password hash. A session cookie keeps you signed in. If you choose Google sign-in, Google provides a verified account identifier and basic profile details such as your email, name and profile picture. Filtrsoft uses these to create or find your account. It does not receive your Google password or request access to Gmail, Drive or your contacts.

Your workspace

Listing reviews, inspection notes, purchase and sale records, product pins, dismissed listings and seller-link clicks are stored with your account. A click to an original seller ad can make that listing eligible for a later outcome question. Opening a Filtrsoft report alone does not. You can dismiss the question or turn off “Ask what happened” in Settings.

Browser preferences, saved listings, recent report history and a device identifier are also kept on your device. Some account and device records are connected when you sign in so earlier work remains available. Clearing browser data does not delete server records. Removing a listing from your feed is recoverable and does not delete the underlying public listing.

Listing research and other services

Filtrsoft processes listing descriptions, specifications, photo references and sale evidence. When image analysis is enabled, listing content and images may be processed by Anthropic. Filtrsoft is hosted on Railway. Operational records help diagnose failures and protect account access. Billing, when used, takes place on Stripe's page; Filtrsoft receives billing status rather than card numbers.

Google's identity service loads on configured sign-in and signup forms. Seller sites, photo hosts and research sites receive normal browser requests when their content is loaded or you follow their links. Those services have their own privacy practices. No advertising or third-party analytics SDK is configured in the app.

Control and retention

Settings lets you manage local history, appearance and follow-ups. Account records and historical evidence may also remain in audit history or backups. Contact the address above for help accessing or deleting account data; clearing local storage or hiding a listing alone is not an account-deletion request.
