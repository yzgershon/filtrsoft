Filtrsoft feed — three directions

Every listing, price, photo, verdict and headline below is live from filtrsoft.com. Nothing is invented: a mockup full of lorem ipsum cannot tell you whether a 68-character Craigslist title wraps badly, and that is exactly what a feed card has to survive.

All three implement Codex's feed rules — sections instead of one mixed list, staleness shown rather than implied, and filtering by unresolved blocker rather than only category and price. The palette, verdict colours and 4/8 spacing are the app's existing tokens, unchanged. What differs is the card language, which is the actual decision.
